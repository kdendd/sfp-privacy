Privacy Policy

Stress Free Painting Gold Coast Last updated: 12 August 2026

Stress Free Painting Gold Coast ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights, in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you request a quote or contact us — including through forms on Facebook or Instagram (Meta lead ads), our website, phone, or email — we may collect:

Your name
Phone number and email address
Your suburb or postcode
Details about your painting project (for example, what you need painted and your preferred timeframe)

We only collect information you choose to give us. We do not collect sensitive information.

How we use it

We use your information to:

Prepare and discuss your free painting quote
Deliver and manage the services you've asked for
Respond to your enquiries
Send you information about our services, where you'd reasonably expect it or have agreed — you can opt out at any time

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How we collect it through Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

If you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta collects your responses and provides them to us. Meta handles your information under its own Data Policy, which is shown alongside our forms. Once we receive your details, this policy applies to how we handle them.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with:

Service providers who help us run our business (for example, our advertising platforms such as Meta and Google, spreadsheet and email tools, and our booking or accounting software), and only so they can perform those services for us
Anyone else, only if you agree or the law requires it

Some of these providers may store information on servers located outside Australia.

How we store and protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to protect it from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then delete or de-identify it.

Access, correction, and complaints

You can ask us at any time to:

See the personal information we hold about you
Correct it if it's wrong
Delete it, where we're able to
Stop sending you marketing

To do any of these, contact us using the details below. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
